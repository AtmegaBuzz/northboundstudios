What kind of agency this is

A consultative AI and automation agency for the travel and tourism industry — not a general web design shop, not a niche wedding-only or adventure-only shop. The positioning is "we audit your operations, find where AI or automation saves cost or unlocks growth, then build the software to do it." Software (websites, apps, systems) is the deliverable that comes out of that process, not the opening pitch.

Who it's for

Two different layers, deliberately kept separate:

Website-level (broad, industry-wide): hotels and resorts, tour operators and DMCs, adventure and activity operators, travel agencies — anyone in travel/tourism whose growth depends on being found online and running efficiently offline.
Sales-target-level (narrow, for now): adventure and activity operators specifically. This came out of the market research — 17.43% CAGR, government infrastructure backing (Swadesh Darshan 2.0), and a buyer demographic (millennials, Gen Z, solo travelers) that's already digital-native and easier to sell automation to than a wedding planner or a resort's procurement team.

The problems being solved, grouped into four buckets

Invisibility — the business doesn't show up in Google search or in AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity), and relies too heavily on OTAs that take 15-22% commission instead of building a direct-booking presence.
Manual operational chaos — vendor and guide coordination happening over WhatsApp threads, spreadsheets, and phone calls instead of a system.
Static pricing — no dynamic adjustment for seasonal demand, so revenue is left on the table in peak periods and undercut in slow ones.
Repetitive manual admin — lead follow-up, review requests, quote/itinerary generation, and contract or invoice paperwork all done by hand instead of automated.

What actually gets delivered

A presence and audience audit (where customers really search, browse, and book, and where visibility is leaking), SEO and AEO work to fix the discovery problem, automated workflows for the operational chaos (scheduling, follow-ups, reviews, documents), and custom software — websites, booking engines, mobile apps, internal tools — built from what the audit actually found, not off a template.

The business model discussed

A low-cost paid audit as the entry point (removes the risk of buying "AI transformation" upfront on faith), which converts into a scoped implementation contract, with an optional ongoing retainer for maintenance and expansion. This part was proposed as a model in the strategy notes you shared, not yet locked in with real pricing.

Credibility behind it

4+ years building web platforms, mobile apps, and AI systems — the basis for "we're not a generic dev shop guessing at AI trends."

Brand identity

Working name: Northbound. Personality: grounded and precise, confident without hype — sits deliberately between "tech agency credibility" and "travel-world warmth," avoiding both the generic AI-startup look and the cliché travel-agency look. Design direction settled on: Modern Editorial — narrative-led layout, serif/sans type pairing, real documentary photography, structured enough to stay credible, warm enough to not feel like a bank.
